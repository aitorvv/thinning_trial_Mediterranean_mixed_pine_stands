## :book: Abstract

This dataset provides multi-temporal dendrometric records (2008-2023) from a long-term thinning trial in a mixed Mediterranean pine forest (*Pinus halepensis* Mill., *P. pinea* L.), complemented by soil and climate data. It offers valuable opportunities for forest modeling and silvicultural purposes. Data accesible at this repository.
  
---

## :file_folder: Repository Contents

- :open_file_folder: [***climodiagrams***](./climodiagrams/): Walter-Lieth climodiagrams for the study site, organized by historical conditions and future SSP climate scenarios (SSP1, SSP2, SSP3, SSP5)
- :open_file_folder: [***data_and_metadata***](./data_and_metadata/): raw data and associated metadata files covering plot coordinates, plot descriptions, soil properties, tree inventories, tree descriptions, historical monthly climate data, and future climate projections
- :open_file_folder: [***graphs***](./graphs/): figures included in the manuscript (stand-level metrics, tree spatial distributions by status and species, study site location map) and interactive plot-level graphs
